My Overview: Management Information Systems (MIS) connect people, technology, and data so that organizations can make smarter decisions. This website explores how businesses use MIS every day to track sales, manage inventory, and share information. Another powerful tool is Artificial Intelligence (AI), which is changing MIS by finding patterns in data much faster than a person could. Along with the benefits, we must talk about ethics, because students and employees can misuse AI by copying work they did not create. Another important topic is security, since hackers and malware threaten the information that every system depends on. I hope visitors leave knowing that responsible use of AI is just as important as the technology itself.
