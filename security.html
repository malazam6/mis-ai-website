Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry: The Ransomware That Shook the World

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware worm began spreading across the globe, locking files and demanding Bitcoin payments to unlock them. It exploited a Windows weakness called EternalBlue and infected more than 200,000 computers in over 150 countries within a few days, including hospitals in the United Kingdom's National Health Service. The attack was significant because it showed how a single unpatched vulnerability could disrupt healthcare and business worldwide, and it pushed organizations to take software updates and backups seriously.
