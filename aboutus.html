Before this assignment, I thought of MIS as just computers, but I learned that it combines people, technology, and data to help managers decide. I also learned that AI can be a great helper, because it explained ideas to me and polished my writing. At the same time, I saw why ethics matters, since copying AI work or hiding AI use is academic misconduct. Researching WannaCry taught me that hackers can shut down hospitals through one unpatched computer. I now understand that security tools like firewalls and multi-factor authentication protect real people. Building this website in HTML showed me how much careful work goes into every page I visit online.
